The four statuses fit together like this:

- **A**: the term is over and everything was paid. This is a good outcome.
- **B**: the term is over and money is still owed. This is a default.
- **C**: the loan is still running and payments are on track.
- **D**: the loan is still running and the customer is recorded as in debt. It is included in the supplied `defaulted` flag; no future status transition is inferred.
